# 03 — KNOWLEDGE
## Research · Documentation · Synthesis · Onboarding · Learning

**Enter-Game Skills · April 2026**

> Knowledge management and deep research account for the largest
> share of agentic AI use in enterprise (McKinsey 2025).
> This domain turns AI into an institutional memory.

---

## USAGE RANK: #1 in IT and knowledge-intensive roles

Critical for: analysts, strategists, consultants, product managers, researchers, technical writers, anyone who needs to know things fast.

---

## SKILLS / COMPÉTENCES

---

### RESEARCH — Deep research on a topic

**Command:**
```
recherche: [topic] · [depth: quick / medium / deep] · [angle: what you want to understand] · [audience]
```

**Output:**
- Executive summary (3–5 lines)
- Key findings with sources
- What's uncertain or contested
- Recommended next reads

**Example:**
```
recherche: tokenomics des protocoles DePIN · medium · comprendre les modèles de revenu · pour pitch investisseur
```

---

### SYNTHESIS — Synthesize multiple sources

**Command:**
```
synthèse: [paste 2–10 documents, articles, or notes] · [question you want answered]
```

**Output:** Synthesized answer to your question, citing which sources support which points. Contradictions flagged.

**When to use:** After a research session, after reading multiple documents, after collecting customer feedback.

---

### BRIEF — Write a briefing document

**Command:**
```
brief: [topic] · [audience: who reads this] · [depth: 1-pager / 3-pager / full] · [context]
```

**Output:** Structured briefing document — context, key facts, implications, recommended actions.

---

### SUMMARY — Summarize a long document

**Command:**
```
résumé: [paste document or URL] · [length: 3 lines / 1 paragraph / 1 page] · [angle: what matters most]
```

**Output:** Summary at requested length, focused on the specified angle.

---

### EXPLAIN — Explain a concept

**Command:**
```
explique: [concept] · [audience: expert / non-technical / executive / new hire] · [depth]
```

**Output:** Clear explanation calibrated to audience. Analogies used for non-technical audiences.

**When to use:** Explaining technical decisions to leadership, onboarding new team members, preparing for a cross-functional meeting.

---

### DOCS — Write technical documentation

**Command:**
```
docs: [what to document] · [type: README / API / runbook / guide / SOP] · [audience] · [context]
```

**Output:** Complete documentation in the requested format. For APIs: endpoint descriptions, parameters, examples. For SOPs: step-by-step with decision points.

---

### WIKI — Write a wiki or knowledge base article

**Command:**
```
wiki: [article topic] · [what the reader needs to know] · [internal context]
```

**Output:** Wiki article with introduction, main sections, and links/see-also section placeholder.

---

### ONBOARD — Create onboarding material

**Command:**
```
onboard: [role: new hire position] · [team] · [key context they need] · [first 30/60/90 days goals]
```

**Output:** Onboarding guide with context, key people, tools, processes, and ramp milestones.

**When to use:** When someone new joins the team. When a role has high turnover. When institutional knowledge lives only in people's heads.

---

### COMPARE — Compare options or technologies

**Command:**
```
compare: [option A] vs [option B] · [criteria: what matters for your decision] · [context]
```

**Output:** Comparison table + narrative recommendation with reasoning.

---

### FAQ — Generate FAQ document

**Command:**
```
faq: [product / process / topic] · [audience: internal / customer / new hire] · [paste existing notes or context]
```

**Output:** FAQ document with 10–20 questions and clear answers. Organized by theme.

---

### INTERVIEW-ME — Pre-decision interview

**Command:**
```
interview-moi sur: [decision or topic]
```

**Output:** 5–8 questions about what you haven't considered — edge cases, trade-offs, hidden dependencies, second-order effects.

**When to use:** Before any significant architectural, strategic, or organizational decision. Takes 5 minutes. Surfaces blind spots consistently.

---

### COMPETITIVE — Competitive analysis

**Command:**
```
competitive: [your product/company] · [competitors: list] · [what you want to understand]
```

**Output:** Competitive analysis with positioning map, differentiators, threats, and opportunities.

## THE INSTITUTIONAL MEMORY ANGLE

The highest-value use of this domain in enterprise is not individual productivity — it's organizational memory.

When team members use `docs`, `wiki`, and `onboard` consistently:
- Knowledge stops dying when people leave
- New hires reach productivity in days instead of weeks
- Decisions have documented rationale that persists beyond the people who made them

This is the compounding effect: each piece of documentation makes the next person faster.

**ROI calculation example:**
- Senior employee exit: 6–18 months to replace institutional knowledge
- 40 hours of `docs` and `wiki` entries: captures 80% of that knowledge permanently
- Cost: 40 hours. Saved: months of ramp time per future hire.
