## WHY THIS EXISTS / POURQUOI CE DOSSIER EXISTE

The data from 2025–2026 is clear:

- **79%** of enterprises face challenges adopting AI despite major investment (Writer, 2026)
- **37%** of AI-saved time is lost to rework — the "AI Tax" (Workday, 2026)
- **Only 14%** of employees consistently get net-positive outcomes from AI
- **51 workdays** lost per employee annually due to AI friction (Futurum, 2026)
- **#1 barrier** to enterprise AI adoption: insufficient worker skills — not technology

The gap is not in the tools. Every company has access to the same models.

The gap is in **how people use them**.

Super-users exist in every organization. They save 2–3 hours a day, produce better output, move faster. But there's no mechanism to spread their practices across the team.

This folder is that mechanism.

## THE ADOPTION PHILOSOPHY / LA PHILOSOPHIE D'ADOPTION

This library is built on three principles:

### 1. Zero-friction onboarding
Every skill in this library works with a single command. No setup, no API keys, no configuration. Say the command, get the output. A new employee should be productive in their first session.

### 2. Universal value
The skills here were selected because they solve problems every knowledge worker has, regardless of industry, seniority, or technical background. If it only works for power users, it doesn't belong here.

### 3. Propagation by proof
The fastest way to spread AI adoption in a team is visible results. When one person saves 2 hours drafting a proposal, their colleague asks how. This library is designed to produce results that are worth sharing — not just saved time, but better-quality output that others notice.

## HOW TO DEPLOY IN AN ORGANIZATION / COMMENT DÉPLOYER EN ENTREPRISE

### Week 1 — Seed with super-users
Share this folder with the 3–5 people in your organization who already use AI effectively. Have them pick their top 3 skills and use them publicly (show outputs in team channels, share the commands in Slack).

### Week 2 — First team training (45 min)
Run a single session: open QUICKSTART.md, show COMMANDS.md, pick 3 skills from this folder relevant to the team's daily work. Live demo > slide deck.

### Week 3 — Mandate one skill
Pick the single highest-value skill for your team (usually `email`, `meeting-summary`, or `status`). Make it the default for one recurring process. Measure time saved.

### Month 2 — Expand by domain
Each team adopts the skills folder relevant to their function. Engineers use `04-CODE.md`. HR uses `05-PEOPLE.md`. Sales uses `06-CUSTOMER.md`.

### Month 3 — Custom skills
Teams add their own commands to their domain file. Internal terminology, specific processes, proprietary templates. The library evolves with the organization.

## THE ALTRUISM ANGLE / L'ANGLE ALTRUISME

This library is open-source and free. Not because it has no value — because the value compounds when more people use it.

When an organization deploys this library:
- Individual contributors save 1–2 hours per day
- Managers spend less time on status-chasing and more on decisions
- New employees onboard faster (weeks → days)
- Knowledge doesn't die when someone leaves

That's not a productivity gain. That's an organizational capability shift.

The goal of Enter-Game is not to help one founder move faster. It's to demonstrate that AI-as-operating-system is accessible to every knowledge worker — not just the 5% who've figured it out on their own.

If this library makes one organization better at what it does, and that organization spreads it to another, the protocol has done its job.
