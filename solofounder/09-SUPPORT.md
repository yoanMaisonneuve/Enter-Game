# 09 — SUPPORT CLIENT
## Onboarding · Success · Retention · NPS · Escalations

**Enter-Game Solofounder · April 2026**

> Your first 100 customers are your best investment.
> Every support interaction is a research session.
> Every complaint is a product spec in disguise.

---

## SKILLS / COMPÉTENCES

---

### ONBOARD-EMAIL — Write a customer onboarding email

**Command:**
```
onboard-email: [product] · [customer type] · [their goal: what success looks like for them] · [step 1 you want them to take]
```
**Output:** Onboarding email sequence (3–5 emails) with: welcome, activation, value moment, habit-forming, check-in.

---

### SUPPORT-RESPONSE — Respond to a support request

**Command:**
```
support: [customer issue: paste or describe] · [resolution available: yes/no/partial] · [tone: empathetic / technical]
```
**Output:** Support response that's clear, human, and action-oriented. Next steps explicit.

---

### ESCALATION — Handle an escalation

**Command:**
```
escalation-support: [what happened] · [customer frustration level: 1–5] · [what you can offer] · [what you can't fix]
```
**Output:** De-escalation response. Acknowledges, owns what's ownable, proposes resolution, next step.

---

### NPS-FOLLOWUP — Follow up on NPS responses

**Command:**
```
nps-followup: [score: detractor / passive / promoter] · [their comment if any] · [what you want to learn or offer]
```
**Output:**
- Detractor: recovery message + invitation to talk
- Passive: value-add + gentle probe
- Promoter: amplification ask (referral, review, case study)

---

### CHURN-SAVE — Save a churning customer

**Command:**
```
churn-save: [customer context] · [reason for leaving: stated] · [what you know about their actual usage] · [what you can offer]
```
**Output:** Churn save message with: acknowledgment, what you heard, what's changing (if true), offer.

---

### SUCCESS-CHECKIN — Proactive customer success check-in

**Command:**
```
cs-checkin: [customer] · [how long they've been a customer] · [their use case] · [what you want to learn or offer]
```
**Output:** Check-in message that's proactive, not reactive. Adds value, opens a conversation.

---

### CASE-STUDY — Turn a customer into a case study

**Command:**
```
case-study-ask: [customer name / company] · [results they've gotten] · [relationship: good / great] · [what you want: quote / full case study / referral]
```
**Output:** Message asking for the case study. Frame that makes it easy for them to say yes.

---
