# 08 — DISTRIBUTION
## Go-to-Market · Channels · Launch · Growth Loops · Partnerships

**Enter-Game Solofounder · April 2026**

> Building is the easy part. Distribution is the hard part.
> Most solo founders build in silence and wonder why nobody shows up.
> Distribution is a system, not a one-time event.

---

## SKILLS / COMPÉTENCES

---

### GTM — Go-to-market strategy

**Command:**
```
gtm: [product] · [target customer] · [problem solved] · [current traction: none / early / some] · [budget: bootstrap / small / funded]
```
**Output:** GTM strategy with: primary channel recommendation, secondary channels, sequencing (what to do first), 30/60/90 day plan.

---

### CHANNEL-AUDIT — Audit your distribution channels

**Command:**
```
channel-audit: [channels you're using or considering] · [current results per channel] · [your time available]
```
**Output:** Channel assessment with: ROI per channel, what to double down on, what to cut, what to test.

---

### LAUNCH-PLAN — Plan a launch

**Command:**
```
launch-plan: [what you're launching] · [audience] · [channels] · [goal: signups / revenue / press / waitlist]
```
**Output:** Launch plan with: pre-launch (build anticipation), launch day (actions by hour), post-launch (amplification). Templates for each channel.

---

### GROWTH-LOOP — Design a growth loop

**Command:**
```
growth-loop: [product] · [how customers currently find you] · [what happens after they use the product] · [what makes them tell others]
```
**Output:** Growth loop design with: input, trigger, action, output, and how to strengthen each step.

---

### PARTNERSHIP — Identify partnership opportunities

**Command:**
```
partnership: [your product] · [your audience] · [what you can offer a partner] · [what you need from them]
```
**Output:** 5–10 partnership types worth exploring, with: what to offer, how to approach, what success looks like.

---

### PRODUCT-HUNT — Prepare a Product Hunt launch

**Command:**
```
producthunt: [product name] · [tagline] · [what it does] · [target hunter type]
```
**Output:** PH launch kit: tagline (< 60 chars), description (< 260 chars), gallery captions, comment responses template, launch day checklist.

---

### PRESS — Write a press release or media pitch

**Command:**
```
press: [news: what happened] · [why it matters] · [quote from founder] · [target: tech press / trade press / local]
```
**Output:** Press release OR journalist pitch email. Angle specific to the target publication type.
