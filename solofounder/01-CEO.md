# 01 — CEO / DIRECTION
## Strategy · Decisions · Vision · Fundraising · Priorities

**Enter-Game Solofounder · April 2026**

> The CEO function is not about doing the work.
> It's about choosing what work matters.
> Every hour you spend deciding wrong costs you a week of execution.

---

## YOUR JOB AS CEO / TON TRAVAIL COMME PDG

As solo founder, the CEO function is the meta-layer above everything else.
It has one output: **clarity on what to do next**.

Without it, you're reactive. With it, you compound.

---

## SKILLS / COMPÉTENCES

---

### VISION — Articulate your vision

**Command:**
```
vision: [what you're building] · [for whom] · [why now] · [what the world looks like when you win]
```

**Output:** Vision statement (1 paragraph), mission (1 sentence), and 3-year north star. Clear enough to share with a potential co-founder, investor, or first hire.

---

### STRATEGY — Quarterly strategy session

**Command:**
```
stratégie: [current state: revenue, users, product stage] · [biggest opportunity right now] · [biggest threat] · [what you've tried] · [horizon: Q / 6mo]
```

**Output:** Strategic priorities for the period. 3 bets maximum. What you're explicitly NOT doing. Rationale.

---

### PRIORITIES — Weekly priority-setting

**Command:**
```
priorités: [everything on your plate: paste your list] · [goal this week] · [constraints: time, energy, dependencies]
```

**Output:** Top 3 priorities ranked. What to defer. What to drop. What to delegate (to AI or async humans).

**Rule:** If you have more than 3 priorities, you don't have priorities.

---

### DECISION — Make a complex decision

**Command:**
```
décide: [decision to make] · [options you're considering] · [constraints: time, budget, reversibility] · [what you already know]
```

**Output:** Decision framework with options, trade-offs, and recommendation. Your biases flagged.

---

### INTERVIEW-ME — Pre-decision interview

**Command:**
```
interview-moi sur: [decision or direction]
```

**Output:** 5–8 questions about what you haven't considered — second-order effects, edge cases, blind spots, risks. This is the single highest-ROI command for strategic decisions.

**Rule:** Run this before any decision that takes > 1 week to reverse.

---

### INVESTOR-UPDATE — Write an investor update

**Command:**
```
investor-update: [period] · [key metrics: MRR, users, growth] · [big wins] · [challenges] · [what you need from investors]
```

**Output:** Investor update in the format investors actually read: brief, specific, honest. Metrics first, narrative second.

---

### PITCH — Prepare a pitch or fundraising narrative

**Command:**
```
pitch: [company name] · [what you do] · [market size + proof] · [traction] · [why you] · [ask: amount, use of funds]
```

**Output:** Pitch narrative with: problem, solution, market, traction, team (solo + AI), ask. Ready to convert into slides.

---

### NORTH-STAR — Define your north star metric

**Command:**
```
north-star: [what your product does] · [who uses it] · [how they get value] · [what you can measure]
```

**Output:** North star metric recommendation with: definition, why it matters, leading indicators, how to track it.

---

### NARRATIVE — Write your company narrative

**Command:**
```
narrative: [company name] · [what you're building] · [the problem in the world you're solving] · [why you]
```

**Output:** Founder story narrative — the "why this, why now, why me" that resonates with investors, early customers, and press.

---

### WEEKLY-CEO — Weekly CEO review

**Command:**
```
weekly-ceo: [what happened this week: wins, misses] · [current metrics] · [what you'll do next week] · [what's worrying you]
```

**Output:** CEO weekly log with: wins (what's working), misses (no judgment), direction check (are we still going the right way), next week plan.

---

## THE CEO MENTAL MODEL / LE MODÈLE MENTAL DU PDG

```
Daily question:  "What's the one thing that, if done today, moves everything forward?"
Weekly question: "Are we moving in the right direction fast enough?"
Monthly question: "Is the model working, or do we need to change something fundamental?"
Quarterly question: "Are we still solving the right problem?"
```

If you can answer these four questions clearly, you're doing the CEO job.
