# 07 — FINANCE
## Pricing · Revenue · Runway · Reporting · Fundraising Prep

**Enter-Game Solofounder · April 2026**

> The solo founder who doesn't track finance is flying blind.
> You don't need an accountant on day one.
> You need a model, a runway, and a pricing strategy.

---

## SKILLS / COMPÉTENCES

---

### PRICING — Set or revise pricing

**Command:**
```
pricing: [product / offer] · [value delivered: what outcome does the customer get] · [alternatives they'd pay for] · [customer: B2B / B2C, size]
```
**Output:** Pricing recommendation with: tiers (if relevant), rationale, psychological anchoring, what to test first.

---

### RUNWAY — Calculate runway

**Command:**
```
runway: [monthly burn: fixed + variable costs] · [current cash] · [revenue: current MRR / ARR if any]
```
**Output:** Runway in months. Break-even point. Scenarios: what happens if MRR grows X% per month.

---

### UNIT-ECONOMICS — Calculate unit economics

**Command:**
```
unit-economics: [CAC: how much to acquire a customer] · [LTV: what a customer is worth over time] · [churn rate] · [margin]
```
**Output:** LTV/CAC ratio, payback period, interpretation, and what to improve first.

---

### FINANCIAL-MODEL — Build a simple financial model

**Command:**
```
financial-model: [business model: SaaS / marketplace / services] · [current metrics] · [growth assumptions] · [horizon: 12 / 18 / 24 months]
```
**Output:** Financial model template with: revenue, costs, gross margin, burn, revenue projections under 3 scenarios.

---

### INVOICE — Write an invoice or billing communication

**Command:**
```
invoice-comms: [client] · [amount + description] · [payment terms] · [overdue: yes/no]
```
**Output:** Invoice cover note OR overdue payment follow-up. Professional, clear, not confrontational.

---

### FUNDRAISING-PREP — Prepare financial materials for fundraising

**Command:**
```
fundraising-prep: [stage: pre-seed / seed / A] · [current metrics: ARR, growth, burn] · [use of funds]
```
**Output:** Financial narrative for investors: what the numbers show, growth rate, capital efficiency, what the round enables.

---

### TAX-BASICS — Understand tax obligations

**Command:**
```
tax-basics: [jurisdiction: province/country] · [business structure: solo / inc / LLC] · [revenue range]
```
**Output:** Overview of key tax obligations for your structure and jurisdiction. Note: verify with an accountant.

---

