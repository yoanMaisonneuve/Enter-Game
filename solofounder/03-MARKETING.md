# 03 — MARKETING
## Content · Brand · SEO · Social · Email · Campagnes

**Enter-Game Solofounder · April 2026**

> Marketing as a solo founder is not about being everywhere.
> It's about being memorable in one place first.

---

## SKILLS / COMPÉTENCES

---

### CONTENT — Write marketing content

**Command:**
```
content: [format: article / post LinkedIn / thread X / newsletter] · [topic] · [audience] · [angle: educational / opiniated / story]
```
**Output:** Ready-to-publish content with headline options.

---

### BRAND-VOICE — Define brand voice

**Command:**
```
brand-voice: [company name] · [who you're talking to] · [3 adjectives that describe your tone] · [what you're NOT]
```
**Output:** Brand voice guide with examples of on-brand vs off-brand phrasing. Reusable for all content.

---

### SEO — SEO content strategy

**Command:**
```
seo: [your product / niche] · [target audience search intent] · [competitors: 2–3] · [what you can realistically publish]
```
**Output:** 10 article ideas ranked by search volume × competition × relevance. Each with suggested title and angle.

---

### NEWSLETTER — Write a newsletter issue

**Command:**
```
newsletter: [audience: who's subscribed] · [this week's topic or story] · [tone: personal / professional / technical]
```
**Output:** Newsletter issue with subject line, hook, body, and CTA. Under 400 words.

---

### LAUNCH — Write a product launch

**Command:**
```
launch: [what you're launching] · [who it's for] · [key benefit] · [channels: email / social / ProductHunt / press]
```
**Output:** Launch kit: announcement email, social posts (Twitter/X, LinkedIn), ProductHunt tagline + description, press paragraph.

---

### AD-COPY — Write ad copy

**Command:**
```
ad-copy: [product] · [target audience] · [platform: Meta / Google / LinkedIn] · [goal: clicks / signups / purchases]
```
**Output:** 3–5 ad variants with headline + body + CTA. A/B test framing included.

---

### POSITIONING — Sharpen positioning

**Command:**
```
positioning: [your product] · [alternatives people currently use] · [your key differentiator] · [who you're for]
```
**Output:** Positioning statement + one-liner + homepage hero copy variant.

---

## QUICK REFERENCE

```
content      → article / post / newsletter by format
brand-voice  → define brand voice

launch       → product launch kit

```
